# ALBUM 02

The second Album for the M-VAVE FM-1: five tracks of filter-house (drums, bass, chords, lead and a singing
voice), three patches each. This is an early build: the sound plays from the keys and over MIDI; the pictures and
the sequencer are still to come.

SELECT picks the track and PRESET its patch. K1 to K4 and ALGORITHM are Tone, Motion, Pump, Sweep and Space; with
EDIT held, K1 to K4 are Phaser, Chorus, Grit and Echo. HOME lets every note go.

Version {{VERSION}}. Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.

- [Try it in the browser](emu/): the same code the FM-1 runs, with sound. No FM-1 needed.
- [Install](install/): from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
