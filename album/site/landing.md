# ALBUM 02

The second Album for the M-VAVE FM-1: five tracks of filter-house (drums, bass, chords, lead and a singing voice),
three patches each, through a bus that pumps on the kick. Every patch has its own moving picture, drawn by code in
the manner of 1970s space-opera animation, moved by the sound and every effect. A sequencer after the KeyStep Pro's
(patterns, scenes, a chain for the song, the arpeggiator, chord mode) and MIDI for playing it from one.
